# 汗 — image sources and licenses

Why 汗: in Japanese it is the character in 可汗 (*kagan*, khagan) and 成吉思汗 (ジンギスカン, *Jingisukan*,
Chinggis Khaan). It is the kanji used for **khan**. On its own, the everyday meaning of 汗 is
"sweat" (*ase*), so a Japanese speaker reading it with no context thinks of sweat first.
Pairing it with a word that gives the context, like the site name, avoids that.

- The OFL covers the font software. An image of a character set in the font is not the font
