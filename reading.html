Dokkai Reader

Read passages aloud and watch the highlight follow your voice. Any reasonable pronunciation is accepted — pause for 3 seconds on a word and its reading and meaning pop up. Finishing a text moves you straight into the next one, and clearing a level unlocks the next.

Needs microphone access and works best in Chrome or Edge — other browsers don't support live speech recognition.
