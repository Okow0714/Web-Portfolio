言葉合わせJapanese Word Match

Connect each Japanese word to its English meaning. Every level has 20 tiles (10 pairs), a 5-minute clock (+20s per pair matched), and 10 levels per JLPT tier — N5 is beginner vocabulary, N1 is advanced. Kanji sharing a phonetic component can also be chained together for a lightning-fast multi-clear. Two mistakes brings a cleared pair back as a penalty, so slow down and look before you click.
