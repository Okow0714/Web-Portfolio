言葉合わせJapanese Word Match

Connect each Japanese word to its English meaning. Every level puts 20 pairs in play on a flat 4-minute clock (+20s per pair matched), and there are 10 levels per JLPT tier — N5 is beginner vocabulary, N1 is advanced. Kanji sharing a phonetic component can be chained together for a lightning-fast multi-clear, and 4 correct pairs in a row banks a powerup you can spend on a free clear or swapping in new words. Two mistakes bring a cleared pair back as a penalty, so slow down and look before you click.
